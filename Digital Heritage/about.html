WHAT IS DIGITAL HERITAGE?

Heritage is more than
what we see.

Digital heritage preservation uses digital methods to document and present cultural places, architecture, traditions and stories.

Through photographs and digital documentation, heritage can be recorded, explored and made accessible to future generations.

02

OUR OBJECTIVE
